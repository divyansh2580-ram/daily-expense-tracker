# Problem Statement
Students and individuals often lose track of their daily minor expenses, leading to poor budget management at the end of the month. 

# Scope of the Project
This project provides a command-line interface to quickly log daily expenses, categorize them, and generate basic financial summaries without requiring complex spreadsheet software.

# Target Users
* College students tracking daily allowances.
* Small business owners logging minor daily operational costs.
* Anyone wanting a simple text-based budget tracker.

# High-Level Features
* Add daily expenses with category and date.
* View a detailed history of all logged expenses.
* Automatically calculate total spending per category.
* Calculate the overall total expense.
